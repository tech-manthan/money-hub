## How the main traffic sources work
- **Etsy ranking:** relevance (title > tags > attributes; first words of the title weigh most) and a *listing quality score* from click-through, favourites, add-to-cart, conversion and review recency. New listings get a short recency boost (reported as 14-21 days). Complete attributes matter ([ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-seo-guide-2026), [SellerToolsHQ](https://sellertoolshq.com/guides/etsy-algorithm-2026/)).
- **Pinterest:** a visual search engine; pins keep earning for months. Signals: domain quality, pin quality (saves, clicks), pinner quality, topic relevance. Fresh pins (new image pointing at a URL) are favoured; 5-15 fresh pins/week is the quoted sweet spot; video pins get extra surface in mobile search ([Tailwind](https://www.tailwindapp.com/blog/pinterest-seo), [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/pinterest-algorithm/)).
- **Short video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts):** now search engines too; repurpose one video with per-platform captions; hooks in the first seconds ([Terra Market Group](https://terramarketgroup.com/blog/digital-marketing-tips-for-social-media-in-2026-turn-reels-tiktoks-and-shorts-into-leads)).
- **Reddit/Facebook groups:** promotion is tolerated only as genuine participation (roughly 90/10), with disclosure; cold pitching and link-dropping get removed ([Redship](https://redship.io/blog/reddit-self-promotion-rules), [MultipleGroupPoster](https://multiplegroupposter.com/blog/facebook-group-promotion-rules/)).

## Implications for the tool
1. Etsy first, then Payhip/Lemon Squeezy/Gumroad as a second storefront; Pinterest + short video as traffic.
2. Quote fees as ranges and tell the user to verify.
3. TPT: warn that AI worksheets are being demoted; only sell reviewed content.
4. KDP: a future export mode (trim sizes, margins, no duplicate interiors).
5. Fresh-pin cadence: generate several distinct pins and videos per product (already done) and a posting schedule.
