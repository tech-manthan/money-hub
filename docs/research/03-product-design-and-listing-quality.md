# 03 - Product quality, print/tablet specs, listing conversion (researched Oct 2026)

## What buyers complain about (so QA should catch it)
From planner reviews and seller forums ([Trustpilot examples](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/my-digitalplanner.com), [Paperlike](https://paperlike.com/blogs/paperlikers-insights/best-digital-planners)): download problems and no support; hyperlinks that do not work; unclear compatibility ("works only in some apps/devices"); landscape layouts that fit iPad Pro but not iPad mini; huge files that lag older tablets; typos and wrong times in PDFs; cover sizing issues; expecting automation but receiving a plain PDF; advertised features that do not exist.
**Rules:** test every link in GoodNotes before listing ([Pink Ink](https://the-pinkink.com/blog/how-to-design-hyperlinked-goodnotes-planner)); never claim features you did not build; state compatibility and "this is a PDF" clearly; keep file size modest; proofread; keep tab positions identical on every page; make tap targets large.

## Tablet planners (GoodNotes / Notability)
- Landscape 4:3 (for example 10 x 7.5 in) works best on iPad; side/top tabs in the same position on every page; the planner must be a PDF with embedded links ([PlanifyPro](https://planifypro.com/blog/guides/digital-planner-goodnotes)). MoneyHub's tablet size is 11.25 x 8.44 in (4:3) with a footer nav on every page. Still to add: side tabs, an iPad-mini safe-area mode, e-ink sizes (reMarkable, Kindle Scribe) - their exact pixel sizes were not confirmed in this research; verify before adding.

## Print specs
- 300 DPI for images; 3 mm (0.125 in) bleed; keep text at least 3-5 mm inside the trim; A4 with 3 mm bleed = 216 x 303 mm; US Letter + 0.125 in bleed = 8.75 x 11.25 in ([Pixel Measures](https://www.pixelmeasures.com/templates/a4-bleed-template/), [Pixels for Print](https://pixelsforprint.com/tools/printable-bleed-margin-checker/)). Home printers cannot print edge-to-edge, so printable planners should keep a safe margin (MoneyHub uses about 0.4 in) and offer no full-bleed backgrounds on home-print versions.
- Binder/disc-bound hole-punch margins were not confirmed in this research; planned option: extra 0.5 in inner margin "binder" variant.

## Wall art
- Standard in 2026: 300 DPI JPEG in five ratios (2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 5:7, ISO/A), each ratio as its own ZIP; Etsy allows 5 files up to 20 MB each ([Elistit](https://elistit.com/blog/etsy-wall-art-sizes-2026-complete-guide/), [SnapToSize](https://snaptosize.com/how-to-package-digital-wall-art-for-etsy)). MoneyHub matches this (pixel sizes chosen so the largest size prints at 150-300 DPI).

## Spreadsheets
- Lock formula cells and leave input cells editable; use data validation (drop-downs); first sheet = "Start Here"; offer both .xlsx and Google Sheets (a PDF with a `/copy` link, since Etsy only delivers static files) ([Sellfy](https://sellfy.com/blog/sell-excel-google-spreadsheets/), [Bricks](https://www.thebricks.com/resources/how-to-sell-excel-templates-on-etsy)). **MoneyHub gap:** sheets are not protected yet and no Google Sheets "copy link" PDF is generated. To build: sheet protection on formula cells and a Google Sheets instruction page.
- Excel 365 checkboxes may not import cleanly into Google Sheets; use drop-down tick marks as a fallback option.

## Worksheets
- Quality means alignment between objective and task, a complete answer key and accurate content; TPT is demoting low-quality AI worksheets ([Chalkbeat](https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/08/03/ai-slop-on-teachers-curriculum-marketplace/), [ISBE alignment unit](https://www.isbe.net/CTEDocuments/FCS-L640136.pdf)). Math sheets in MoneyHub are generated algorithmically with computed answers; AI-written content needs human review.

## Listing photos and video
- First image becomes the thumbnail: landscape/square, subject centred with padding; recommended 3000 px, minimum 2000 px on the shortest side; keep each image under about 1 MB or uploads may fail; up to 20 photos allowed, the commonly advised plan is 10 ([Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-listing-photo-size-guide/), [Alura](https://www.alura.io/docs/article/etsy-shop-image-optimization-guide)). Listings with video are reported to convert 20-30% better (vendor claim, unverified).
- Listing video: 5-15 s, at least 1080 px, MP4/MOV up to 100 MB, aspect ratio between 2:1 and 1:2, audio is removed so the story must work silently ([Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/how-to-add-a-video-to-an-etsy-listing/)). MoneyHub's 1920x1080 video fits; a 15 s cap check is to add; images should be compressed under 1 MB (to add: JPEG quality step-down).

## Pricing and bundles
- Bundles 20-35% under the sum of parts; Etsy "Buy Together" minimum 5%; check that the discounted bundle still nets more than singles after fees; tiered offers (single, pack, full bundle) anchor the middle option ([MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/digital-product-bundles-a-profitable-etsy-strategy/), [Margin for Makers](https://marginformakers.com/guides/how-to-price-digital-products-on-etsy)).

## Seasonality
- List seasonal items 6-9 weeks before the peak; Christmas products live by 1 Oct (15 Sep for competitive categories) ; back-to-school: list from mid-June, peak July-August; late listers capture a fraction of early listers' sales; 20-40% of annual revenue for many printable shops lands in Nov-Dec ([Listify AI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-holiday-calendar-2026), [Dynamic Mockups](https://dynamicmockups.com/etsy/prepare-etsy-shop-for-q4/)).
