# 06 - Marketing content, short video, hashtags, ads, Canva and KDP (researched Oct 2026)

## Pinterest pins
- 1000x1500 (2:3). Safe zone: keep text and CTAs away from the **bottom 20%** (Save button/profile overlay) and edges (about 50 px); the centre 60% is safest ([PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/pinterest-aspect-ratios), [Pinterest Create](https://create.pinterest.com/fundamentals/)). MoneyHub pin layouts place the headline in the top or middle and the product-name line near the bottom: **adjust** so the product line sits above the bottom 20% (to do in the pin layouts).
- Video pins: 6-15 s performs best, start with ~10 s; state the outcome in on-screen text in the first 3 s; many viewers watch muted ([Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/pinterest-videos/), [84Pins](https://84pins.com/pinterest-video-pin-specs/)). Pinterest recommends 2-5 hashtags in the description; keywords in titles/descriptions matter more than hashtags.

## Short video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)
- ~70% decide within 3 seconds; strong retention at 3 s earns many more impressions; a workable structure is hook (3 s), core (about 15 s), CTA (about 7 s); 21-34 s is the optimal range for most content; **69% watch muted and burned-in captions raise retention 15-25%** ([Teleprompter](https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/tiktok-3-second-rule), [Sprout](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-video-statistics/)). Vendor figures; treat as direction, not law.
- Instagram caps posts at **5 hashtags** (since Dec 2025); keyword-rich captions beat hashtag stuffing; on TikTok use 3-5 niche tags and skip #fyp ([Funnl](https://funnl.ai/do-instagram-hashtags-work-in-2026/), [Dive Media](https://www.divemedia.com.au/marketing-tips-and-insights/social-seo-keywords-vs-hashtags)).
- The `/brag` approach (below) matches this: 15-25 s, hook in the first 2 s, every line readable (about 0.3 s per word), every frame postable, music + SFX mixed, a poster frame and a postable caption.

## Listing copy
- Open with the strongest keywords and the main benefit (the first ~160 characters show in search); then benefits (3-5 bullets), file formats and quality (for example 300 DPI), a digital-only disclaimer ("No physical item will be shipped"), and an FAQ; short paragraphs ([Growing Your Craft](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/etsy-product-description-templates), [MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/how-to-optimize-your-etsy-listings-to-sell-more-digital-products/)). MoneyHub's descriptions already follow this; the opening paragraph should repeat the title keywords.

## Advertising
- Etsy Ads suit proven bestsellers (4-5%+ conversion), seasonal pushes (2-3 weeks before peak) and new-listing launches ($2-3/day for 1-2 weeks); digital items can break even at ROAS 1.5-2; $1/day is too little to learn from ([InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-ads-cost-alternatives), [Listify AI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-ads-worth-it-2026)). Do not advertise before organic data shows a listing converts.

## Canva (if you ever offer editable templates)
- Allowed if the design is transformative; Pro elements only via a **template link** (not "Copy link"); safest to use only free elements and fonts; do not resell Canva's own templates/elements ([Canva Help](https://www.canva.com/help/using-canva-to-create-products-for-sale/), [LicenseOrg](https://www.licenseorg.com/blog/canva-license-explained)). Not implemented; our products are PDF/XLSX/JPG.

## Amazon KDP (paperback interiors)
- Min 24 pages; no-bleed interiors use a PDF the exact trim size; outside margins at least 0.25 in; **gutter by page count: 24-150 pp 0.375 in, 151-300 0.5, 301-500 0.625, 501-700 0.75, 701-828 0.875**; fonts embedded; common trims 6x9 (default), 8.5x11 (activity), 5.5x8.5 (compact journals) ([KDP guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201857950), [Ceeryo](https://www.ceeryo.com/blog/kdp-book-margins-and-gutter-guide), [SmartKDP](https://smartkdp.com/guides/kdp-margins)). Disclose AI-generated text/images to KDP; avoid duplicate interiors. A `moneyhub kdp <id>` export (planner/journal/checklist/worksheet interiors, gutter-aware) is a good future feature.

## Trend research workflow (free)
1. Etsy search autocomplete in a private window; write down suggestions weekly. 2. Pinterest Trends and Google Trends for seasonality and style. 3. Look for ideas with high interest and a modest number of competing listings. 4. Validate with 3-5 real listings before scaling ([InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/how-to-find-trending-products-etsy), [imgSEO](https://imgseo.io/blog/pinterest-trends-product-research-guide)).
