# 05 - Risks, operations and realistic benchmarks (researched Oct 2026)

## Realistic expectations
- Income is highly skewed: about 65% of Etsy sellers make under USD 100/year; the top 1% take 30%+ of sales ([Printful](https://www.printful.com/blog/etsy-statistics), [Listify AI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make-2026)). Vendor figures for active digital shops (USD 800-5,000/month with 100+ listings) are survivor-biased. First sale commonly takes 3-8 weeks; about 165 days to the first USD 1,000 in one data set. Plan for months and for many listings, not a few.
- **Conversion benchmarks** (vendor-reported): Etsy search 2-4%, Etsy ads 1-3%, external traffic 0.5-2%, repeat customers 5-15%; new shops 0.5-2% ([eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/what-is-a-good-conversion-rate-on-etsy/), [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-conversion-rate-benchmarks)). MoneyHub's report page should show views -> favourites -> sales so you can compare.
- **Star Seller** (rolling 3 months): 95% message response within 24 h, 4.8+ rating, 5+ orders and USD 300+ sales; digital shops are exempt from shipping/tracking ([Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/how-to-become-etsy-star-seller)).

## Biggest risks and mitigations
| Risk | Evidence | Mitigation in MoneyHub |
|---|---|---|
| Shop suspension for missing AI disclosure or mass near-identical listings | [ShieldMyShop](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-03-26-etsy-suspension-2026-new-rules-what-changed) | Disclosure in every description; uniqueness fingerprint; style/seed variation; build a **listing-pace guard** (warn when listing many near-identical products) |
| Trademark/brand terms in titles, tags, content | [Etsy banned words](https://www.etsy.com/market/banned_words_list), [Unflagged](https://unflagged.store/blog/trademarked-words-on-etsy): entertainment, sports, and "secret" trademarks (Onesie, Velcro, Band-Aid); "inspired by" does not help | **Risk check** against a built-in watch list + your own list |
| Buyer cannot print correctly | Common complaint; "Actual size / 100%" vs "Fit to page" ([Plain Plan](https://www.plainplan.co/planner-guide/print-pdf-poster-adobe-acrobat/)) | "Start Here" PDF with printing steps, paper size, compatibility |
| Broken links / typos / false feature claims | See 03 | QA checks and claims review |
| File sharing/piracy | Watermarks cut theft 60-80% (vendor claim); watermarks hurt Etsy photo eligibility ([Thrive on Etsy](https://thriveonetsy.com/how-to-protect-your-etsy-digital-products-from-piracy/)) | Licence text inside every download; no watermark on listing photos; optional per-buyer watermark is not possible on Etsy static files |
| Pinterest labels/filters AI content | [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/pinterest-labeling-tag-ai-generated-images-pins/741986/) | Pins are built from product renders and procedural art; label AI backgrounds |
| Copyright of AI output | [US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/) | Template-driven, human-reviewed products; keep your edits (editable SVG workflow) |
| VAT/sales-tax mistakes off Etsy | [Stripe](https://stripe.com/resources/more/uk-vat-on-digital-products) | Prefer merchant-of-record storefronts (Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy) for non-Etsy sales |
| Weak freebies/funnels | Lead-magnet research ([OptinMonster](https://optinmonster.com/9-lead-magnets-to-increase-subscribers/)) | `moneyhub sample` makes a free sample PDF; pins say what the freebie is |

## Operations checklist (per product)
1. Open every file on a phone, a laptop and (planners) in GoodNotes; click every link.
2. Print one page at "Actual size" and check margins.
3. Read the listing aloud; remove claims the product cannot back.
4. Run `moneyhub risk <id>`; fix anything flagged.
5. Upload; set price with a launch discount; add the video; save as draft; re-read tomorrow.
6. Post 5-6 fresh pins over a week (different headline and image each).
7. Log views/favourites/sales weekly; after 4-6 weeks keep, tweak or retire.
8. Answer buyer messages within 24 hours; fix any broken download immediately (cases are open for 100 days after first download).
