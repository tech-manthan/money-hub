# 02 - Policies, legal and compliance (researched Oct 2026)

Not legal advice. Policies change; always read the platform's current page. Primary sources are linked where I found them.

## Etsy
- **AI disclosure is mandatory** when generative AI was used in the item or listing images. You must put the disclosure in the **listing description** and switch the "who made it" attribution from *Made by* to **Designed by** the seller. AI-assisted designs directed by the seller (product type, style, palette, keywords, your own prompts) are allowed under the Creativity Standards. **Selling AI prompts or prompt bundles is prohibited**, and you must write your own prompts ([Etsy seller handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004), [PromptlessPress](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-etsy-ai-policy-2026-digital-products), [Inkfluence](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-explained-2026)).
- **Enforcement:** shops that generate large numbers of near-identical listings, or omit disclosure, are being suspended, sometimes without warning and with held funds ([ShieldMyShop](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-03-26-etsy-suspension-2026-new-rules-what-changed), [PixPipe](https://pixpipe.app/en/blog/etsy-suspending-ai-sellers)). Etsy also enforces creativity standards more consistently and removes duplicates/stolen content ([iscompliant.app](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-creativity-standards-pod-sellers-guide)).
- **IP:** brand names, characters, logos and celebrity names are takedown triggers even as "inspired by" or fan art; resold free downloads/fonts/clipart cause strikes; Etsy follows DMCA and removes listings on valid notices; repeat violations lead to bans ([Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-copyright-infringement-explained/), [Etsy IP guidance](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1075628311049)).
- **Refunds:** Etsy's standard stance is no refunds on digital downloads, but buyers can open quality-based cases (corrupted, blurry, missing pages) for 100 days from first download or 12 months from purchase; the buyer must download the file first ([GoldCity Ventures](https://goldcityventures.com/how-to-handle-customer-service-for-digital-product-sales-on-etsy/)). Fast, friendly fixes protect your Customer & Market Experience score.
- **Taxes:** Etsy collects US sales tax as a marketplace facilitator on qualifying digital orders; you still owe income tax, and obligations outside Etsy's collection remain yours ([Zamp](https://zamp.com/blog/etsy-collect-sales-tax-sellers)).
- **API:** Etsy Open API v3 has three access tiers; Commercial Access needs an approved Personal App plus manual review. File upload is optional when creating digital listing *drafts* since 29 Jan 2026 ([etsy/open-api #1524](https://github.com/etsy/open-api/discussions/1524), [VorpLabs](https://vorplabs.com/agent-tools/etsy-api)). A draft-only publisher for your own shop is feasible; publishing stays a human step.

## Pinterest
- Pinterest labels AI-generated/modified pins ("AI modified") from creator selection, IPTC metadata or its own classifiers, lets users see fewer AI pins, and has further policy updates effective 12 Nov 2026 ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/pinterest-labeling-tag-ai-generated-images-pins/741986/), [Zernio](https://zernio.com/blog/pinterest-new-ai-content-filter)). Community Guidelines apply the same whether AI or not. MoneyHub pins are composed from real product renders and procedural art, but label AI-generated imagery where Pinterest asks.

## Copyright and licensing
- US Copyright Office (Jan 2025): works made solely by AI are not copyrightable; AI-assisted works are protectable only for the human contribution; prompts alone are insufficient ([Jones Day](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/02/copyrightability-of-ai-outputs-us-copyright-office-analyzes-human-authorship-requirement), [copyright.gov/ai](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/)). Practical effect: your layouts, curated content and edits are what you can protect; do not assume exclusivity over pure AI art. This is one more reason the products here are template-driven with human review.
- **Licence terms:** buyers expect clear personal-use terms; sellers often offer separate commercial licences that forbid redistribution of the original file ([Etsy examples](https://www.etsy.com/market/commercial_license_digital_products)). MoneyHub adds a personal-use licence to every product ("Start Here" page).

## Tax and VAT
- Non-EU/UK sellers owe VAT from the first sale on B2C digital services unless a merchant of record handles it; EU-based sellers have a EUR 10,000 cross-border threshold and a new EUR 100,000 SME exemption ([Stripe](https://stripe.com/resources/more/uk-vat-on-digital-products), [FrameKit](https://framekit.ai/blog/vat-on-digital-products-for-creators-2026)). Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy are merchants of record (they collect and remit tax), Payhip handles EU/UK VAT only, Sellfy/Stan Store do not ([getly](https://www.getly.store/blog/gumroad-vs-lemon-squeezy)). This matters if you sell outside Etsy.

## Teachers Pay Teachers and KDP
- TPT uses algorithms to demote stores with low-quality AI content ([Chalkbeat](https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/08/03/ai-slop-on-teachers-curriculum-marketplace/)).
- KDP requires private disclosure of AI-generated text/images (not mere AI assistance); plain grids/tracking tables generally do not; "duplicate interiors" are flagged ([KDP Builder](https://kdpbuilder.com/blog/kdp-ai-disclosure-rules)).

2. Never put prompts on sale (done: no prompt products).
3. **Risk check** on titles, tags, descriptions, pin text and content: trademark/brand/celebrity/character terms, "inspired by", income/health/guarantee claims, free-font resale phrases. (To build.)

5. Personal-use licence + support/how-to page inside every download (to build).
6. Pin/video cadence guidance with fresh creative, no identical reposts (guides done; calendar to build).
