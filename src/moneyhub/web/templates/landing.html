Questions people ask

I don't know what to sell. Can I still use this?

Yes. Step 1 shows ready-made audiences (nurses, teachers, new parents…) and a Surprise me button. The Ideas page brainstorms more.

Will it make me money?

No tool can promise that. MoneyHub saves you the making. Whether a product sells depends on your audience, your listing and your promotion. Start with a few small tests.

Does it post to Etsy for me?

No, on purpose. You review each product and upload it yourself, which keeps you in control and within each shop's rules.

Do I need an AI account or paid keys?

No. It works fully offline with built-in content. If you have Claude Code or an API key, the writing gets more tailored to your audience.

Can I use the command line instead?

Yes. Everything in the app also works from a terminal. See the command-line guide.
