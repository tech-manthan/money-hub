Quick start

  1. Open Make a product.
  2. Press Surprise me (or pick an audience), then Next twice, then Make my product.
  3. When it's done, open it from the green button and look through the pages.

That's the whole loop. The rest of this page explains each choice.

Page layouts: ready-made or generated

Planners come in more than one page layout, because buyers like different things. In step 3 you pick from three kinds:

  • Ready-made layouts are hand-tuned and reliable. Weekly planner: classic, grid, focus, minimal. Daily planner: hourly, priorities, time-block.
  • Generated layouts. Press Generate another layout and MoneyHub invents a new arrangement (boxes left, right, top or bottom, days as a list, grid or columns, extra habit or priority blocks). There are thousands. Every picture is a real page, so what you see is what you get.
  • Describe it. Type what you want, such as "big priorities on the left and a habit row at the bottom". Your AI helper turns that into a layout. Without an AI helper you still get a generated layout.

Generated layouts are checked automatically. If one would not fit on a page, MoneyHub falls back to the classic layout and tells you.

moneyhub layouts weekly_planner --count 8          # picture of presets + 8 generated layouts
moneyhub make "nurse" weekly_planner --page-layout grid
moneyhub make "nurse" weekly_planner --page-layout auto:42     # layout number 42: the same every time
moneyhub make "nurse" daily_planner --layout-prompt "no schedule, long to-do list, notes at the bottom"

Your brand colour and e-ink tablets

  • Brand colour. In step 2 choose I'll pick, then Use my own brand colour. We build a matching set of colours and keep the text readable. Command line: --brand "#2F6B57".
  • E-ink. For reMarkable, Kindle Scribe and Boox, tick the e-ink box in step 3. Pages are black and white with crisp lines, sized 3:4, with tap-to-jump links. Command line: --eink. Works with the weekly and daily planners, journal, checklists, worksheets and guides.
  • Where you'll sell. Tick the shops in step 3. We make listing pictures and an upload guide only for those.

After it's made

  • Where the files are. In the out/products/ folder, one folder per product: product/ (what customers get), listing/ (text), images/ (listing photos and pins) and upload-package.zip.
  • Make a new variation keeps the audience and product but changes the look and wording.
  • Editing. Use the editable files option on the product page to adjust text and positions in a design tool.
  • Track results. Record when you list a product and when it sells. The Sales & learning page shows which audiences, products and looks work.
  • Launch videos. moneyhub brag (or the product page) makes a short video with music and share text for Pinterest, Reels and TikTok.

Sell it somewhere else: KDP, Notion, Canva

On a product's page, Sell it somewhere else makes a ready-to-use pack, each with a step-by-step guide.

  • Amazon KDP paperback. A print-ready interior at an exact book size (6 x 9 in and others), margins that meet KDP's rules for the page count, a cover template with the spine width worked out, and a KDP guide. Always confirm the spine width with KDP's own cover generator.
  • Notion. A zip of pages and databases that Notion imports. After importing, switch columns to Checkbox or Date as you like. Notion cannot import formulas.
  • Canva. The pages as a PDF and images for Canva's import, a brand kit (colours and fonts), a "your template link" page for buyers, and a how-to. Canva does not accept template files from outside, so you finish the template inside Canva and paste its link into the buyer page.
moneyhub export kdp 4 --trim 6x9 --paper white
moneyhub export notion 4
moneyhub export canva 4 --link "https://www.canva.com/design/..."

Check each platform's current rules before selling: KDP content guidelines, Notion's and Canva's template-selling terms.

Connect AI services (Settings)

Open Settings to add keys. None are required. Keys are saved on your computer in a hidden file that is not committed to git, shown only as the last four characters, and sent only to the service they belong to. Each has a Test button and a Remove button.

  • Writing helpers: Claude Code (uses your login, no key), Anthropic, Google Gemini (free tier), Groq (free tier), OpenRouter, and local Ollama.
  • AI artwork for wall art: claude_svg lets Claude draw vector art with no image key. Hugging Face, Together AI, OpenAI or your own image server make pictures instead. Check each service's terms before selling images it makes.
  • Natural narration: moneyhub voices install adds a natural voice for launch and tour videos.
  • Optional video engines: moneyhub engines install hyperframes (HTML to video), remotion, or manim. Run moneyhub doctor to see what is installed.

Everything also works from the command line

Run source .venv/bin/activate first. Then:

moneyhub make "nurse" weekly_planner               # one product
moneyhub make "nurse" wall_art --palette plum --fonts classic --motif arches --page-layout framed
moneyhub styles                                    # every look and layout option you can choose
moneyhub layouts weekly_planner                    # picture of page layouts to choose from
moneyhub next                                      # what to make next, learned from your sales
moneyhub types                                     # product types, audiences, AI helpers
moneyhub ideas --focus pets                        # brainstorm what to make
moneyhub batch "nurse,teacher" --products weekly_planner,habit_tracker
moneyhub list                                      # your products
moneyhub regenerate 3                              # new variation of product 3
moneyhub approve 3                                 # mark it ready
moneyhub risk 3                                    # brand/claim check on the listing text
moneyhub listed 3 etsy https://etsy.com/... --price 6.5
moneyhub sale 3 etsy 6.5                           # record a sale
moneyhub report                                    # what's working
moneyhub bundle 3,4,5 --name "Nurse Bundle"        # sell products together
moneyhub sample 3                                  # free preview PDF
moneyhub export kdp 3                              # also: notion, canva
moneyhub brag 3                                    # launch video + share text
moneyhub calendar                                  # seasonal opportunities
moneyhub keywords "nurse planner"                  # search phrases to check by hand
moneyhub doctor                                    # check your setup
moneyhub ui --port 8766                            # this web app

Shop rules MoneyHub helps with

  • Etsy AI disclosure. Listing descriptions include the line Etsy asks for. Keep it.
  • No brand names. The risk check flags trademarked and brand terms in titles and tags. Fix them before listing.
  • Pinterest safe zone. Pin text stays out of the bottom 20%, where Pinterest overlays its own buttons.
  • Quality first. A product is only "ready" when the quality checks pass.
  • No income claims. Don't promise buyers or yourself specific earnings.
  • Check the current rules. Marketplace rules change. Read the shop's own help pages before you list.

Plain-English glossary

Niche / audienceThe group of people a product is for, such as nurses or new parents.
PrintableA file the customer prints at home. They download it; nothing is shipped.
PaletteThe set of colours used across the product.
Seed / look numberA number that fixes the random choices, so the same number gives the same look.
Listing kitThe title, description, tags and pictures for your shop page.
Upload packageA zip with the files to upload, plus guides.
QA (quality checks)Automatic checks for problems such as text that doesn't fit or missing files.
PinA Pinterest picture that links to your listing.
Offline modeMaking a product without any AI service, using built-in content.

Troubleshooting

  • "Address already in use" when starting. Another program uses that port. Run moneyhub ui --port 8766.
  • Something won't generate. Run moneyhub doctor. It lists what is missing and how to fix it.
  • It's slow. AI helpers add time. Untick "Also make short videos" in step 3, or use --llm builtin on the command line.
  • PDF or fonts look wrong on Mac. Make sure Homebrew's pango is installed (brew install pango), then rerun setup.
  • Preview images are missing. Open the product page again; previews are made on first view.

What it can't do yet

  • It does not upload to any marketplace. That is by design.
  • Not built yet: Amazon KDP export for cards, wall art and spreadsheets; page layouts for the other product types (planners have them today).
  • Not yet verified on a real device or app: how the exports behave inside Canva, Notion, KDP's previewer, GoodNotes links and e-ink tablets. Spreadsheet recalculation in Excel and Numbers.
  • Spelling checks flag possible mistakes as warnings. They never block a product.
  • Ideas and keyword suggestions are educated guesses, not live sales data. Check them by hand on Etsy and Pinterest.
